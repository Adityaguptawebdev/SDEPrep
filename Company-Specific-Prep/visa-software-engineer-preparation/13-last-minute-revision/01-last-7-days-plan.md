# LAST 7 DAYS BEFORE THE VISA INTERVIEW

> Assumes the OA is cleared and interviews are ~7 days away (~5–6 focused hours/day). If your OA is still pending, do Day 7 → Day 6 twice with the [mock OAs](../02-online-assessment/04-mock-oa-sets.md) first.

**Easy analogy — shaadi se pehle ka hafta**: Naya kuch nahi seekhna, jo hai usko pakka karna, dress trial (mock) karna, aur last din aaram.

## Interview day checklist
- ☐ Laptop charged, stable internet, camera/mic tested (virtual) — or pen, paper, ID, printed resume (onsite, "no laptop" rounds were reported).
- ☐ Excalidraw / a whiteboard tool open and tested for drawing architecture.
- ☐ Water, 10 minutes early, recruiter's phone number saved (interviewers joining late was reported — stay calm, call the recruiter after ~10 minutes).
- ☐ For every coding question: clarify → brute force → optimal → code → dry run → complexity.
- ☐ After each round: note the questions (for the next round and for this repo).

**🗣️ Aakhri din yaad rakho**: "Mujhe har cheez nahi aani chahiye — mujhe apna project, HIGH-frequency DSA, Java/Spring basics aur 6 sachchi stories pakki aani chahiye."
